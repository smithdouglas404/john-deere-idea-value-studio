# Brightidea research notes — 2026-08-18

Sources reviewed:

1. https://www.brightidea.com/product/
2. https://www.brightidea.com/solution/hackathon/

Verified observations from the official Brightidea product page:

- Brightidea presents a broad innovation suite including Whiteboard, Idea Box, Memo, Program, Hackathon, Lab, Ecosystem, and Transformation.
- The product page also lists multiple use-case modules/activities including Department, Innovation Process, Discuss, Explore, Hackathon, Optimize, Pitch, Recognize, Solve, Slash, Monitor, Apply, Understand, Incubate, Accelerate, Scout, Venture Fund, Host a Public Event, Gig, Consult, Transform, and Suggest.
- The page positions Brightidea as a centralized enterprise innovation-management platform rather than only a hackathon tool.

Verified observations from the official Brightidea hackathon page:

- Brightidea describes a setup-to-judging workflow intended to reduce event-planning guesswork.
- It claims automation, collaboration tools, and real-time analytics for organizers, participants, and judges.
- The page explicitly highlights judging management, a tablet judging experience, and a top-proposals dashboard for discovering and evaluating ranked/categorized projects.
- The page markets Brightidea as a scalable solution for organizing hackathons and delivering measurable business impact.

Assessment boundary:

- These notes record official positioning claims and visible feature labels. They do not independently verify every connector, implementation detail, customer outcome, pricing figure, or post-event investment workflow.
- The John Deere Idea Value Studio comparison must treat Brightidea's breadth and maturity as strengths, while evaluating our differentiation on transformation-oriented community assessment, investment traceability, governed proof, repository audit, evidence-bounded specialist analysis, and human transformation decisions.
