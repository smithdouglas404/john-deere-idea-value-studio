Illustrative activation

Start small, learn fast,
leave with an operating cadence.

A focused first field shows the client how crowdsourced signals, AI-assisted value cases, and accountable decision gates work together before a broader rollout.Bounded activation · not a delivery commitment
Six-week field-to-fund activation
One priority domain · one visible portfolio · one recurring gate
WEEK 01

Align

Define one priority domain, the decision criteria, value taxonomy, participant map, and guardrails.

Tangible outcomeSponsor alignment and an agreed field boundary
WEEK 02

Capture

Open the signal field across selected employee, dealer, customer, and partner channels.

Tangible outcomeCurated signals with traceable sources and emerging themes
WEEK 03

Shape

Use AI and domain expertise to refine the leading opportunities into comparable value cases.

Tangible outcomeProblem statements, value ranges, and evidence gaps
WEEK 04

Prove

Run micro-sprints that test the assumption most likely to change the decision for top candidates.

Tangible outcomeTargeted demonstrations and an evidence trail
WEEK 05

Field

Build the first investment view: comparable cases, confidence levels, delivery fit, and named sponsors.

Tangible outcomeA governed idea-to-investment portfolio
WEEK 06

Decide

Facilitate the first gate. Advance, reshape, pause, or close each case with a documented rationale.

Tangible outcomeFirst decisions plus a repeatable operating rhythm
What remains
A practical cadence for turning the next wave of signals into value cases—without returning to a one-off event model. The portfolio becomes the product.
Scope note: Priority domain, participant groups, data access, governance controls, and commercial assumptions should be defined jointly with John Deere before activation.
