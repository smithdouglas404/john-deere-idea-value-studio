Responsible AI operating model

AI accelerates the value work; people retain the judgment.

The co-pilot’s job is to make the work of finding, structuring, and testing opportunities faster and more legible. It should never make a funding decision opaque.

Illustrative AI rationale

What supports the case: recurring field signals, service patterns, and a defined affected segment.

What remains uncertain: data readiness, sponsor capacity, and the proof required to validate the value range.

Design every AI contribution to expose its source, logic, confidence, and missing evidence.

Operating moment
AI co-pilot contribution
Human accountability
Capture01 / SIGNAL
Cluster and clarify incoming signals
Group related observations, identify duplicates, and draft a neutral problem statement.
Choose what deserves attention
Leaders and domain experts judge strategic relevance, source quality, and customer context.
Shape02 / VALUE CASE
Draft the value hypothesis
Surface likely benefits, affected stakeholders, comparable patterns, and key assumptions.
Own the economics and trade-offs
Sponsors validate the range, risk tolerance, and the conditions that make the case viable.
Prove03 / EVIDENCE
Organize proof and summarize learning
Recommend evidence gaps, generate experiment materials, and distill results for review.
Interpret evidence in operating reality
Teams determine whether evidence is sufficient, transferable, safe, and ready for progression.
Decide04 / GATE
Prepare a transparent decision record
Present assumptions, confidence, open risks, and the basis for the recommended next action.
Fund, reshape, pause, or stop
The accountable sponsor retains the final decision and the responsibility for its outcome.
