Success measures

Measure the pipeline, not just the participation.

Use a small, repeatable set of indicators that reveal whether signal is becoming evidence, evidence is becoming accountable decisions, and decisions are producing value.

01 / EARLY SIGNAL

Leading indicators

Are we finding the right opportunities and making them legible quickly?

Signal diversity across employee, dealer, customer, and ecosystem sources

Signal-to-theme synthesis time

Time to an initial, source-linked value case

Share of candidates with a named sponsor

Field quality
02 / GOVERNED PROGRESSION

Decision indicators

Are we learning fast enough to make a clear, accountable portfolio choice?

Evidence-gap closure rate by candidate

Elapsed time from signal to investment gate

Advance, reshape, pause, and stop decisions documented with rationale

Share of portfolio value linked to validated evidence

Decision clarity
03 / REALIZED BENEFIT

Outcome indicators

Are advanced cases creating durable customer, operating, or strategic value?

Validated customer or operating benefit from advanced cases

Rework and low-confidence investment avoided

Adoption, renewal, or retention support where relevant

Capacity reallocated away from low-evidence initiatives

Value realized
Measurement principle
A better idea program is not a louder innovation event. It is a clearer path from experience to value to decision.
