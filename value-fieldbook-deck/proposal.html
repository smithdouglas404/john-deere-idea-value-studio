The proposal

Introduce the Value Fieldbook.

A continuous, AI-assisted operating model that retains broad participation while giving every promising idea an evidence-led route to the next investment decision.From crowd signal → capital decision
01 / OPEN THE FIELD

Capture

Invite employees, dealers, customers, and ecosystem partners to contribute grounded frictions and opportunities—not pre-baked solutions.

OutputTraceable field signals and source context
02 / MAKE IT LEGIBLE

Shape

Use AI to cluster patterns, sharpen the problem, identify affected stakeholders, and draft a transparent value hypothesis.

OutputComparable opportunity and initial value range
03 / TEST WHAT MATTERS

Prove

Run focused micro-sprints on the assumption most likely to change the decision—customer pull, economics, feasibility, or ownership.

OutputEvidence trail, confidence level, and gaps
04 / GOVERN THE MOVE

Decide

Review the value case through a recurring investment gate. Advance, reshape, pause, or stop with a named sponsor and next action.

OutputAccountable portfolio decision
Operating principle
The output is not “the best hack.” It is a portfolio of decisions with a visible route from experience to value to action.
