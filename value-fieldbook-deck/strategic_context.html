Strategic context

Deere already builds AI around practical customer value.

Public signals that matter for the innovation model
500Kconnected machines

Connected operations create an unusually rich signal field.

John Deere reports 500,000 connected machines and more than 70 million processed messages per day—an operating context where high-potential insights are too distributed and fast-moving for purely manual synthesis.

2026startup collaborator cohort

Innovation already combines internal and ecosystem capabilities.

Deere’s 2026 cohort spans advanced sensing, analytics, automation, AI-driven robotics, and digital crop intelligence, each oriented toward practical benefits in agriculture and construction.

AIacross the journey

Value is expected before full commitment.

Deere describes applying AI across the customer journey and proving customer benefit—an approach that translates naturally to how innovation cases should earn their next investment.

Proposal implication
The opportunity is not to introduce AI into innovation. It is to make the route from signal to value visible and governable.

Use AI as a transparent co-pilot that helps teams surface, structure, test, and explain opportunities while leaders retain the judgment to fund, reshape, or stop them.
