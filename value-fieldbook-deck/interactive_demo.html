What the client experiences

One working conversation, four clear moves.

01

Add a field signal

A contributor records an observed friction or opportunity without needing a fully formed solution.

02

Inspect the opportunity

The team sees the stage, source signals, initial value range, confidence, and next decision.

03

Reveal the rationale

AI makes the logic, assumptions, and missing evidence visible rather than supplying an opaque score.

04

Advance the gate

Leaders move a case toward sponsor review—or reshape or stop it—with a documented rationale.

Prototype note: sample ideas, value ranges, and confidence scores are illustrative placeholders for discussion—not John Deere forecasts or commitments.
