The value bottleneck

The build is no longer the bottleneck. Decision-quality is.

Generative tools can make the event faster. They do not automatically make the investment case clearer.

Traditional emphasisProposed emphasis
01 / PrepareChallenge designManual briefs, rules, onboarding
02 / FormIdeateTeams, whiteboards, early concepts
03 / BuildPrototypeNow heavily compressed by AI
04 / Value workDefine · validate · decideCustomer problem, value range, evidence, delivery fit, ownership
01

Is this problem important enough?

Ground the case in a real customer or operating friction—not an available technology.

02

What range of value is credible?

Declare the economics and assumptions before a polished demonstration creates false certainty.

03

What proof would change the decision?

Focus scarce sprint time on the riskiest assumption rather than on feature completeness.

04

Who owns the next move?

Name the sponsor, investment gate, decision date, and condition for advance, reshape, or stop.

Implication: Don’t use AI to produce more hackathon artifacts. Use it to produce a faster, more accountable route to value.
