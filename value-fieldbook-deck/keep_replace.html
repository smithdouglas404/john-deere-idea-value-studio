Operating model shift

Preserve participation.
Change the unit of progress.

Keep the access, collaboration, and momentum of crowdsourcing. Replace event-day scoring with a visible path to evidence and a decision.

Keep from the hackathon
Replace with the Value Fieldbook
Keep

Broad access to ideas and diverse voices

Employees, dealers, customers, and partners can all contribute grounded observations.

Change

Always-on signal capture across the ecosystem

Every contribution retains its source, context, and relationship to an emerging opportunity.

Keep

Rapid collaboration and prototype energy

Teams benefit from focused time together and permission to explore.

Change

Evidence-focused micro-sprints

Short cycles test the assumption most likely to change the investment decision—not feature completeness.

Keep

Friendly competition and visibility

Good ideas deserve exposure beyond the team that first raised them.

Change

Transparent progression criteria

Value, evidence, delivery fit, and named ownership determine what advances.

Keep

A focal moment for leaders

Leadership attention is valuable when it is connected to a meaningful next move.

Change

Recurring investment gates

Each case receives a decision to advance, reshape, pause, or stop—plus a sponsor and date.

The output
is a decision

Do not reward the best hack. Build a portfolio of value cases leaders can govern.
