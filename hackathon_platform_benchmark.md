# Hackathon Platform Benchmark and Build Roadmap

## What leading platforms establish as the baseline

The current market establishes a clear operating baseline: event registration, team formation, project submission, configurable judging, a leaderboard, and organizer reporting. Devpost positions its product around centralized registrations, projects, judging, voting, and outcome measurement for public, internal, and customer events.[1] TAIKAI additionally makes the live-event layer explicit through matchmaking, an event timeline, internal chat, real-time leaderboard, rich submissions, voting, and external chat/livestream integrations.[2] HackerRank Engage focuses on AI-assisted event creation, coding challenges, audience growth, and developer-community engagement.[3]

| Platform | Verified strength | What it does not make central | Implication for Value Fieldbook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Devpost | Registration, projects, judging, voting, permanent galleries, adoption/impact reporting | Evidence provenance, proof validation, sponsor-owned decision gates, post-event value realization | Match polished event operations; differentiate with evidence-to-investment continuity. |
| HackerEarth | Hosted event operations, coding challenges, progress and judge workflows, analytics/integrations | Investment underwriting, longitudinal value validation, evidence-first decision governance | Add operational maturity without turning the product into recruiting software. |
| HackerRank Engage | AI-assisted event setup, technical challenges, developer engagement and funnel analytics | Business-case, customer-impact, sponsor-economics, and human investment decision workflow | Use AI to accelerate preparation and evidence work, not to replace decision authority. |
| TAIKAI | Matchmaking, timeline, chat, real-time leaderboard, submissions, voting, livestream/community integrations | Structured opportunity value case and non-binding evidence audit tied to post-proof realization | Match the live-event experience; differentiate with governed proof and outcome tracking. |
| Major League Hacking | Organizer playbooks, registration/check-in, mentoring culture, judging plan standards | Enterprise portfolio governance, opportunity economics, structured evidence validation | Bring strong participant care and event operations into the governed investment journey. |

## The deliberate differentiator

Value Fieldbook should **not** compete merely by collecting registrations and naming winners. Its operating advantage is the chain from: contributor evidence → AI-supported structured brief → sponsor-entered value case → public research with limitations → governed community signals → controlled proof sprint → cited technical/specialist evidence → human scoring → recorded realization indicators.

> AI can accelerate discovery and evidence review, but it cannot set value, select winners, or approve investment. Sponsor and human decision authority remain visible at every gate.

## Immediate capability roadmap

| Priority | Capability | Why it matters | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Live Event Pulse**: a single organizer view of team readiness, evidence completeness, audit queue state, judging readiness, and current blockers | Brings real-time operational clarity normally scattered across registrations, submissions, chat, and judging tools | Build next from existing event, project, audit, team, and score records. |
| 2 | **Participant mission control**: milestone checklist, team status, submission completeness, mentor/office-hour routing, and visible evidence requirements | Makes the product feel like a guided proof experience instead of a form collection exercise | Partially present; consolidate into one participant-facing workflow. |
| 3 | **Mentor and judge operations**: office-hour sign-up, conflict/recusal view, reviewer workload, concise evidence packets, and reviewer calibration | Establishes participant support and fair, fast decision operations | Judge assignment and recusal exist; scheduling and calibration view need expansion. |
| 4 | **Live event communication**: announcement acknowledgement, team-specific alerts, and event timeline changes | Matches the operational experience expected from leading live-event tools | Announcements, schedules, and team messages exist; add a unified live pulse surface. |
| 5 | **Portfolio continuation**: proof milestones, owner, next action, indicator evidence, and decision history after the event | Extends beyond the usual leaderboard into a true investment operating system | Realization exists; make the transition and ownership more prominent. |

## Build principle for the next increments

First match the market’s excellent event experience—clear onboarding, collaboration, timely updates, team support, and real-time operational visibility. Then preserve the distinct Value Fieldbook advantage: all insights are linked to evidence, AI outputs are provisional, sponsorship economics are human-entered, and a hackathon is a proof point rather than the end of the story.
