09 / Sponsor authorization

Authorize a bounded proof sprint.

Fund the evidence needed for the next decision—not a contest for its own sake. A 4–6 week sprint should deliver a working signal-to-action path and an accountable recommendation.

Scope

One focused workflow.

Use approved test data to demonstrate a traceable condition signal, service context, and next action.

Accountable roles

Sponsor + service + user panel.

Name an accountable sponsor, service/operations subject-matter expert, representative users, and proof-sprint owner.

Decision-ready package

Evidence to hold, refine, pilot, or advance.

Deliver cited technical evidence, observed workflow feedback, documented constraints, pilot measures, and a sponsor-owned recommendation. AI findings remain advisory; humans retain the gate.

Success is not “winning the hackathon.” Success is reducing uncertainty enough for an accountable next investment decision.
