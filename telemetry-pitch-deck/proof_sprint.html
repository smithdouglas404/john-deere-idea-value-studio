06 / Controlled proof sprint

A hackathon should prove the hypothesis—not simply perform a prototype.

The sprint produces a functional telemetry-to-action demonstration and a decision-grade evidence package. The success condition is a better next investment decision.

01 / SIGNAL

Use authorized test data.

Work only with synthetic, consented, or approved telemetry evidence and record its limitations.

02 / WORKFLOW

Show the action path.

Make the operator/dealer service flow traceable from condition signal to accountable next step.

03 / EVIDENCE

Test the system boundary.

Document connectivity, mixed-fleet, data-quality, and human-adoption constraints as evidence—not excuses.

04 / DECISION

Package the proof.

Deliver cited technical evidence, user feedback, and a human-reviewed recommendation to hold, refine, pilot, or advance.

Pilot measures to collect

Signal precision · time-to-triage · avoidable coordination steps · operator acceptance · service-team readiness.

Test boundary

No claim of fleet-wide savings or ROI without field pilots, service logs, and sponsor validation.
