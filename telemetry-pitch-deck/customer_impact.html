03 / Customer and operating hypothesis

A clearer handoff can matter to every actor around the machine.

The intended experience is a shared operational picture—not an automated decision. Each benefit remains a hypothesis until it is observed in a representative workflow.

FARM OPERATOR

Earlier context, less uncertainty.

Receive a clearer explanation of a machine condition and the next accountable service action during a time-sensitive field window.

DEALER / SERVICE TEAM

Prepare before dispatch.

Review condition, operating context, and known limitations before reactive triage or a field visit.

JOHN DEERE ECOSYSTEM

Link signal, service evidence, and outcome.

Create a governed trail connecting the machine signal to the action taken and the evidence of its result.

What the proof must establish

Customer interviews, service-log comparison, pilot telemetry, and user observation must show whether these handoffs improve a real workflow. No satisfaction, downtime, or financial outcome is assumed in advance.
