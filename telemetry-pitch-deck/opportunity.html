01 / Opportunity hypothesis

Convert a machine signal into an informed service action.

The opportunity is a bounded telemetry workflow that connects actionable machine patterns, service context, and an accountable next action before a disruption becomes extended downtime.

OBSERVED FRICTION

Time-sensitive field work leaves little room for reactive triage.

Operators, dealers, and service teams need a clearer shared context when a machine condition may require attention.

WORKING WORKFLOW

Signal → evidence → serviceable next step.

Package the condition, operating context, and known limitation so a qualified person can decide what action to take.

VALUE PERSPECTIVE

Cost optimization through less avoidable coordination friction.

This is a qualitative mechanism—not a verified savings, revenue, or ROI claim.

Sponsor validation required

Proof question: will a specific farmer/dealer workflow act on the signal often enough to create measurable value?
