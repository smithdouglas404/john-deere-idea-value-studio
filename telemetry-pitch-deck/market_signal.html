02 / Cited market validation

The capability signal exists. The business case is still a proof question.

Public sources establish connected-machine and precision-agriculture precedent. They do not establish farmer willingness to adopt this workflow, field-level economics, or a validated ROI.

John Deere Operations Center

Positions a system to plan, monitor, analyze, and share farm data. [1]

John Deere JDLink

Describes connected-machine and remote-diagnostics capabilities relevant to service workflows. [2]

USDA ERS precision-agriculture adoption

Supports segmentation by farm characteristics rather than a blanket adoption assumption. [3]

VALIDATION GAP

Can a real operator and dealer act on this signal?

That answer requires pilot telemetry, service-log comparison, interviews, and an observable action path—not a vendor claim or a prototype demo.

Proof implication: test a narrow customer and dealer workflow before expanding the scope.
