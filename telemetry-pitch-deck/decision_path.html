08 / Field-to-fund decision path

Evidence before escalation.

The hackathon is a controlled proof point inside a longer operating system. Every stage creates context for the next human decision.

01 / EVIDENCE

Opportunity signal

Voice, documents, contributor observations, and a working problem statement are retained with provenance.

02 / VALUE

Sponsor boundary

People enter the value range, proof cost, time horizon, assumptions, and current investment gate.

03 / RESEARCH

Cited validation

AI organizes public precedent, source links, limitations, customer gaps, and questions to investigate.

04 / PROOF

Controlled sprint

Teams build a testable workflow; agent and specialist evidence remains cited and provisional.

05 / DECISION

Human gate and realization

Human scorecards, sponsor rationale, next actions, and indicator evidence determine whether to hold, refine, pilot, or advance.

Decision rule

No community vote, AI output, specialist score, or leaderboard position becomes an investment decision. Each may inform a human review; only the sponsor and accountable decision makers move the gate.
