## Reconciliation notes

The source set is implemented as one operating journey rather than as 17 disconnected screens. Opportunity intake creates the evidence ledger, sponsor economics forms the working value canvas, a hackathon becomes the controlled proof mechanism, and human judging plus recorded indicators drive the post-proof decision rail. The documented exceptions are infrastructure substitutions only: managed scheduler/database-queue operations replace a standalone Redis/Celery deployment pattern, while versioned MySQL/TiDB cosine retrieval replaces native PostgreSQL `pgvector`/HNSW until that stack is explicitly required.

No source is treated as authority for an autonomous award, investment, hiring, legal, IP, or security decision. The application preserves cited evidence, participant challenge, sponsor ownership, and human final authority throughout the reconciliation.
